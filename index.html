HYPER TETRIS — GUIDE

What's a slice?

Higher dimensions (4D / 5D / 6D) extend the well along extra axes (w, v, u). The bright cyan well in the centre is the slice you're playing in; the faint outlined wells beside / in front / above it are neighbouring slices — w → left/right, v → front/back, u → up/down. A piece that crosses a slice boundary visibly extends into the next well. Walk through slices with [ ] (w), , . (v), - = (u).

Hyper-clear

A line clears when an entire hyperplane is full. The more dimensions, the bigger the score multiplier — but pieces also fall slower. Clear 4+ planes at once for a HYPER bonus.

Hold

Press Tab to stash the current piece and pull from hold. Only once per piece — useful to save an I-piece for a deep clear.

Camera

Drag with the mouse to orbit, scroll to zoom.Drag the view with one finger to orbit, pinch to zoom. The camera auto-centers when you switch modes. The game pauses while this guide is open.
